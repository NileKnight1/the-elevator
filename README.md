# The Elevator

# Gameplay
 There are 3 modes in the game, each on has a tutorial inside, a quick view:
 - Story mode: You're locked in an apartment. You have to collect the car parts scattered through the apartment. When you complete the car parts, you take the car and escape. You can hide in the wardrobe or behind the sofa. Some weird things will happen!
 
 - Floors Mode: You're locked the same apartment. You have two options, whether you go up or go down. You should check whether the apartment looks as usual or something has happened sdas. If something has been changed, you shouldn't go down. If something has been changed, you shouldn't go down. Don't make mistakes.
 
 - Fix Mode: You're locked in the same apartment. In this mode you observce anomalies events, like object's position isn't as usual. Click on it and choose 'Position' to change its position. And now it's in its place. You might find something is missing. Click on its place thne choose 'Show'. And if something is new to place. Click then hide it. You have to finish before the time, then take the elevator to the garage. You must leave at maximum 3 anomalies to be able to go to the garage, unless that you'll restart.

# Controls
- A-D moving
- Space - Jump
- Shift - Sprint

# Devices
- Supports touch and non touch devices
